About TechEdu

Empowering the next generation of web developers, one student at a time

Our Story

Founded in 2026 by a group of passionate computer science students, TechEdu Learning Hub was born from a simple observation: web development textbooks were either too expensive, too complicated, or both.

We started as study partners struggling through our APT 1040 course, creating simple notes and examples to help each other understand HTML tables, CSS layouts, and JavaScript functions. When our classmates started asking for copies of our notes, we realized we had something valuable to share.

Today, TechEdu has grown into a comprehensive learning platform used by hundreds of college students. But our mission remains the same: make web development education accessible, practical, and even fun.
